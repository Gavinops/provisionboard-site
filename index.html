Provision Board

Useful things. Trusted people. A stronger community.

Provision Board is a private, invite-only board for households and communities. Sell, trade, lend, or give things away. Stay in touch with your circle, share what you need, and help one another through everyday life or an outage.

Private, invite-only · Testnet beta · No real-money payments

  • No public sign-up. Invites are single-use; in-person invites expire after 24 hours.
  • You choose who sees what, where you meet, and when. No home addresses.
  • AI suggests and drafts. You decide what gets posted or sent.

How it is built

Four layers, each one simple on its own.

Sharing stays simple: an item card, a circle you trust, location choices, and testnet-only payments.

Item cards

Snap a photo. AI suggests the name, condition, section and a typical used-price range. Every AI field is labeled as a suggestion you can edit. Your real photo stays the photo: the AI never generates, edits or replaces it.

Trust layer

Circles of people you know, and grants that say who can see or take what. Stop sharing with one person, one circle, or everyone at once, in one tap.

Location

Choose Dark, Rough area, or Meet-only. Share only what you choose, never a home address.

Money

Testnet only, with play money. The wallet design uses Xaman so members keep their own keys. Live payments stay off.

What does the AI look at when I snap a photo?

It identifies the item and suggests a title, condition (New, Like new, Good, Used – works, Fair, or For parts / repair), section and a typical used-price range. If it guessed the condition, the label says so and you tap to fix it. It never touches payments and never sends anything.

Is there a minimum price?

Paid items start at $5. Free listings have no price, fee, or checkout.

Trust layer

Share with the people you trust, and stop in one tap.

Make a circle for your household, neighbors, church, or another group you know. Invite-only access keeps the board closed to strangers. You decide exactly what each circle can see or take.

Shared lockers

Your Locker is private storage for things you own. Share a locker with a circle so members can take or reserve items.

Reserve with a pickup date

Members reserve an item with a pickup date and a note. You confirm, then mark it picked up. Reservations that pass their time release on their own.

Group offers with caps

Post an offer to a circle with a capacity and a per-person limit. Everyone sees the slots left, and the offer closes when it is full.

How do I stop sharing?

Open Sharing & privacy. You can stop a single grant, stop sharing with one person or circle, or tap "Stop sharing with everyone". It takes effect right away.

Can I put a time limit on a share?

Yes. When you grant access you choose how long it lasts.

How do invites work?

There is no public sign-up link. The owner invites members privately. Each invite can be used once, and in-person invites expire after 24 hours.

Is SOS an emergency service?

No. SOS alerts your group; it does not contact emergency services. Hold to send, and choose whether to include a location. Location is off by default.

What if two offline updates conflict?

Both versions are shown so the group can sort it out. One version does not silently overwrite the other.

When the connection goes quiet

Hive Mode. Keep helping, even in an outage.

A home computer can keep the board running for invited members over local Wi-Fi without internet. When a working connection is available, members can also connect through a private encrypted network.

Local Wi-Fi, familiar board

Use the board, chat, share needs and offers, and arrange free claims with people on the same local network. Remote access still needs a working connection.

Phones helping nearby phones

“Pass messages to phones next to you” is off by default. One toggle turns the phone-to-phone back channel on or off; advanced controls stay with the owner.

Private messages stay encrypted

Private messages travel only over the encrypted phone-to-phone path, not a radio relay.

During outages, digital payments are off. Use cash or trade in person only.

Do I have to turn on the phone-to-phone back channel?

No. It starts off and stays off unless you choose “Pass messages to phones next to you”. The owner can disable member features too.

Is the radio relay available now?

Not yet. A radio relay for long-range messages is planned under Coming soon. Private messages use only the encrypted phone-to-phone path.

Location and map

One setting. Never a home address.

Location is a single choice, and you can change it at any time.

  • Browse a rough area you choose. Cards show approximate distances and public meetup spots, not home locations.
  • Map pins sit only at meetup lots or snapped rough areas, never at a seller's home.
  • Meet at a public lot in daylight. No first meet at a home.

Dark

No location is stored and location sharing stops. You can still browse and search by a rough area you choose.

Rough area

People or circles you pick see an approximate area, never your address.

Meet-only

Share one public meetup spot with one person, only during the time you pick. It is deleted right after.

Does Provision Board store my GPS position?

No street address is ever accepted. If you tap "use my current location", it is snapped to a rough area. In Dark mode nothing is stored at all.

What map does it use?

OpenStreetMap tiles, with the map code served from Provision Board itself rather than a third-party CDN.

Money

Testnet only. Your keys stay yours.

Digital payments are for testing with play money only. The design uses your own Xaman wallet; Provision Board never holds funds or has your keys. No live payments are enabled.

One approval checkout

The testnet design groups the seller payment and platform fee into one wallet approval. Nothing is paid without your approval.

Escrow and pickup codes

The testnet design locks funds on the ledger, not with the host. A pickup code releases them; a set refund time lets the buyer recover them.

RLUSD and XRP

The payment design supports RLUSD checkout and XRP escrow. For now, all payment use is limited to the testnet with play money.

Fees are waived during the beta. Mainnet stays off pending an outside security audit and legal review.

What are the fees?

Fees are waived during the testnet beta. The current design lists a 2.3% online platform fee with a $0.50 minimum, and 0.5% for in-person sales. Free listings have no fee. Any live fee schedule will be confirmed after legal review.

What is Xaman?

Xaman is the phone wallet app members use. Provision Board sends Xaman a request, and you approve or decline it on your phone. Xaman is mobile-only.

What if I lose my pickup code?

In the testnet design, escrow is released with the pickup code or returned to the buyer after the set refund time. Live payments remain off.

AI agents and the Ask helper

AI suggests. You decide.

AI assist follows you throughout the app. The Ask helper can open screens, set your search filters, open a listing and write drafts. Everything else comes back as a draft you edit, or a button that takes you to the screen where you tap.

What AI can do

Identify an item from your photo, suggest a condition and price range, navigate, filter, and draft messages and listings.

What AI can't do

It never posts, pays, signs, claims, reserves, sends, or changes sharing or location without your tap.

Agent keys

Agent keys are scoped (list, search, offer, draft) and revocable at any time. A revoked key stops at once, and every action shows in your action log.

Does the AI ever see my payment keys?

No. Your keys stay in Xaman. The AI never touches payments.

Safety and privacy

Private by design. Easy to stay in control.

  • Meet at a public lot in daylight. No first meet at a home.
  • No home addresses. Pins are meetup spots or rough areas, and distances are rounded.
  • Stop sharing with everyone in one tap.
  • You hold your own keys. Provision Board never holds funds.
  • Report a listing or a member from the app.

Security tested in repeated test-and-fix rounds before every release.

Is this live with real money?

No. Payments are testnet only, with play money. Mainnet stays off pending an outside security audit and legal review. During outages, digital payments are off: cash or trade in person only.

Can anyone join?

No. The board is private and invite-only, with no public sign-up. Invites are single-use, and in-person invites expire after 24 hours.

Can I give things away?

Yes. The Free section is built, including free claims in Hive Mode. There is no price, fee, or payment checkout for free items.

What stays under the owner’s control?

Every member feature has an owner off switch. The ten communication tools each have their own on/off switch in Settings under Communication. The phone-to-phone back channel is off by default.

The lightweight Android app is about 7 MB. It is shared privately with invited members, not through app stores. The latest build includes all ten communication tools.

By invitation, not by search

iPhone comes later

The iPhone app is planned for a later release. Android is the current private app build.
