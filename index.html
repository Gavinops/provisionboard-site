Provision Board

Your idle goods, moving to whoever needs them, on your terms.

Provision Board is a peer-to-peer local marketplace and sharing board on the XRP Ledger. People own idle goods that should move to whoever needs them: sold, traded, lent to a circle, or given away. You decide who sees what, where you meet, and when.

Here is how it fits together, one simple layer at a time.

How it is built

Four layers, each one simple on its own.

Every listing and every share runs through the same four layers: the item card, the trust layer, location, and money.

Item cards

Snap a photo. AI suggests the name, condition, section and a typical used-price range. Every AI field is labeled as a suggestion you can edit. Your real photo stays the photo: the AI never generates, edits or replaces it.

Trust layer

Circles of people you know, and grants that say who can see or take what. Stop sharing with one person, one circle, or everyone at once, in one tap.

Location

One setting: Dark, Rough area, or Meet-only. Search by ZIP and radius. Never a home address.

Money

Pay in Xaman from your own wallet. One approval covers the whole checkout, on the XRP Ledger.

What does the AI look at when I snap a photo?

It identifies the item and suggests a title, condition (New, Like new, Good, Used – works, Fair, or For parts / repair), section and a typical used-price range. If it guessed the condition, the label says so and you tap to fix it. It never touches payments and never sends anything.

Is there a minimum price?

Yes. The minimum item price for a sale is $5, checked on the phone and on the server.

Trust layer

Share with the people you trust, and stop in one tap.

Make a circle for family, neighbors, church or any group. Invite people by handle. Then decide exactly what each circle can see or take.

Shared lockers

Your Locker is private storage for things you own. Share a locker with a circle so members can take or reserve items.

Reserve with a pickup date

Members reserve an item with a pickup date and a note. You confirm, then mark it picked up. Reservations that pass their time release on their own.

Group offers with caps

Post an offer to a circle with a capacity and a per-person limit. Everyone sees the slots left, and the offer closes when it is full.

How do I stop sharing?

Open Sharing & privacy. You can stop a single grant, stop sharing with one person or circle, or tap "Stop sharing with everyone". It takes effect right away.

Can I put a time limit on a share?

Yes. When you grant access you choose how long it lasts.

Location and map

One setting. Never a home address.

Location is a single choice, and you can change it at any time.

  • Search by ZIP and a radius. Cards show an approximate distance in whole miles, the town, and the public meetup lot.
  • Map pins sit only at meetup lots or snapped rough areas, never at a seller's home.
  • Meet at a public lot in daylight. No first meet at a home.

Dark

No location, ever. Nothing is stored and every location share stops. Search by typing a ZIP or town.

Rough area

People or circles you pick see about where you are (about 2 km, or your ZIP area), never your address.

Meet-only

Share one public meetup spot with one person, only during the time you pick. It is deleted right after.

Does Provision Board store my GPS position?

No street address is ever accepted. If you tap "use my current location", it is snapped to a rough area. In Dark mode nothing is stored at all.

What map does it use?

OpenStreetMap tiles, with the map code served from Provision Board itself rather than a third-party CDN.

Money

Self-custody payments with Xaman.

You pay from your own wallet, and the seller is paid to theirs. Provision Board never holds funds and never has your keys.

One approval checkout

An XRPL Batch bundles the seller payment and the platform fee so they all happen or none do. You approve once in Xaman.

Escrow and pickup codes

For pickups, money can be locked in escrow on the ledger itself, not held by us. The buyer's pickup code releases it to the seller at the meetup. After the refund time, it can be returned to the buyer.

RLUSD and XRP

Checkout uses RLUSD, a US-dollar stablecoin issued by Ripple. Escrow is XRP-only today, because RLUSD's issuer has not enabled token escrow.

Beta status: Provision Board runs on the XRP Ledger testnet with play money. Mainnet is built but switched off, pending attorney review.

What are the fees?

As stated in the current design docs: online sales carry a 2.3% platform fee with a $0.50 minimum, added on top of the price. In-person sales carry a 0.5% fee. An optional 0.5% builder fund is off unless enabled and is pending attorney review. During the beta, fees are waived. The final fee schedule will be confirmed after attorney review.

What is Xaman?

Xaman is the phone wallet app members use. Provision Board sends Xaman a request, and you approve or decline it on your phone. Xaman is mobile-only.

What if I lose my pickup code?

Escrowed money can only be released with the pickup code, or refunded to the buyer after the refund time that was set when the escrow was created.

AI agents and the Ask helper

AI suggests. You decide.

AI assist follows you throughout the app. The Ask helper can open screens, set your search filters, open a listing and write drafts. Everything else comes back as a draft you edit, or a button that takes you to the screen where you tap.

What AI can do

Identify an item from your photo, suggest a condition and price range, navigate, filter, and draft messages and listings.

What AI can't do

It never posts, pays, signs, claims, reserves, sends, or changes sharing or location without your tap.

Agent keys

Agent keys are scoped (list, search, offer, draft) and revocable at any time. A revoked key stops at once, and every action shows in your action log.

Does the AI ever see my payment keys?

No. Your keys stay in Xaman. The AI never touches payments.

Safety and privacy

Built to keep strangers safe.

  • Meet at a public lot in daylight. No first meet at a home.
  • No home addresses. Pins are meetup spots or rough areas, and distances are rounded.
  • Stop sharing with everyone in one tap.
  • You hold your own keys. Provision Board never holds funds.
  • Report a listing or a member from the app.
Is this live with real money?

No. It is a testnet beta with play money. Mainnet stays off until attorney review and a small real-money dry run.

Do I need an account?

You sign in with Xaman. New members install Xaman and write their recovery words on paper first.

Can I give things away?

Yes, from your Locker. A dedicated Free section with search is coming soon.
